For any question, bug or suggestion: suporte@casaderaiz.pt

I reply in Portuguese or English. This is one person's personal project, not a company with a support desk – a reply usually takes a few days.

Before you write

An email with these four things saves a round trip:

  • the app version, under Mais → Sobre (More → About);
  • your device model and iOS or iPadOS version;
  • what you were doing, and what happened instead of what you expected;
  • a screenshot, if there is anything to see.

Please don't send backups or photos of invoices. I don't need your data to understand a bug, and I don't want to hold it.

Frequently asked

Where does my data live?

On your device. An obra you keep to yourself never leaves it; an obra you choose to share also lives on a server in the European Union, where only that obra’s members can read it. The details are in the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. The whole app works without one, and that is where it starts. The account exists for sharing an obra with whoever is building it with you: until you choose to share one, the obra stays on your phone alone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, under Mais → Conta → Apagar a conta (More → Account → Delete account): it is deleted there and then, and the screen says first what happens to each obra you are in. Or write to suporte@casaderaiz.pt from the account’s own address, and it is deleted within 30 days. The email, the name and any obra you are the last member of are deleted. If you run an obra other people are in, you choose first whether to hand it to one of them or delete it for everyone. The obra saved on your phone is untouched – if you want that gone too, uninstall the app, and export the backup before you do.

How do I make a backup?

Under Mais → Cópia de segurança (More → Backup), the app exports a single file containing every record and attached file, and hands it to the share sheet. Keep it off the phone – Files, iCloud Drive, email, wherever you prefer. On a shared obra, attachments you have never opened on this phone are only in the cloud; before exporting, the app says how many there are and offers to download them first.

I changed phones. How do I bring the build with me?

Export the backup on the old device, install the app on the new one, and import the file from the same screen. Records and attachments both come back.

I deleted the app without a backup. Can you recover it?

If the obra was only on the phone, no – and it isn't a matter of willingness: there is no copy of it on the developer's side. If iPhone backup was enabled, the iOS backup may have included the app's storage; that is the only possibility, and it is managed in your system settings. An obra that was shared comes back when you install the app and sign in to the same account.

Does the app calculate IMT or Imposto do Selo?

No, and it never will. Both are banded, both vary by property type and region, and both change most years – a figure the app computed would be believed because it is a number. The app opens the field; you type what the assessment says.

Which VAT rate should I use?

The app offers the list of legal rates and lets you choose; it does not tell you which one applies to your case. That question is for your accountant, or for the contractor issuing the invoice. Nothing in this app is tax, legal or engineering advice.

Does it work offline?

It does. Everything you write is saved to the phone first, and nothing waits for a network to happen – on a site with no reception the app works just the same. A connection is only needed to create an account, to sign in, and for shared obras to catch up between phones.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad.

What about Android?

Not for now. If that changes, it will be said here.

Suggestions

It is a tool for someone managing their own build.
