For any question, bug or suggestion: suporte@casaderaiz.pt

I reply in Portuguese or English. This is one person's personal project, not a company with a support desk – a reply usually takes a few days.

Before you write

An email with these four things saves a round trip:

  • the app version, under Sobre (About), in the circle in the top-left corner – on a computer, in Ajuda → Sobre (Help → About);
  • your device model and iOS or iPadOS version – or, on a computer, the browser and system;
  • what you were doing, and what happened instead of what you expected;
  • a screenshot, if there is anything to see.

Please don't send backups or photos of invoices. I don't need your data to understand a bug, and I don't want to hold it.

Frequently asked

Where do I get the app?

On the App Store, for iPhone and iPad. It is free. On a computer there is nothing to install: it opens at app.casaderaiz.pt.

Does it work on a computer?

Yes, in a browser, at app.casaderaiz.pt. It is the same app, with the same build: sign in with the account you use on the phone and it is there. A few things are different, on purpose:

  • An account is required, and the build has to be in the cloud. A build that is only on a phone is saved to the cloud first, on that phone; then it appears on the computer. A build can also be created on the computer, and is in the cloud from its first moment – the one thing on the computer that needs a connection to happen.
  • Browsers: Chrome, Edge and Firefox, in recent versions, and Safari on the iPad. Safari on a Mac should work, but there is no Mac to try it on – if something fails there, say so.
  • One tab at a time. The build is open in one tab only; opening it in another asks whether to move it there.
  • Signing out deletes this browser’s copy of the build, after sending whatever is still to send. The build stays in the cloud and on the phones; to see it again, sign in again.
  • Reminders stay on each person’s phone, chosen there. The computer shows the same list of what is to do, and sends no notifications.
  • A backup is not restored on a computer. You can download one, but the computer’s build always comes from the cloud, and restoring it would delete the other people’s work in it.

Where does my data live?

On your device. A build you keep to yourself never leaves it; a build you choose to share also lives on a server in the European Union, where only that build’s members can read it. On a computer, the browser keeps a copy of the build until you sign out. The details are in the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

On the phone, no. The whole app works without one, and that is where it starts. The account exists for sharing a build with whoever is building it with you: until you choose to share one, the build stays on your phone alone. On a computer, yes – it is the way in.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, under the circle in the top-left corner, in Conta → Apagar a conta (Account → Delete account) – on a computer, in Definições → Conta → Apagar a conta (Settings → Account → Delete account): it is deleted there and then, and the screen says first what happens to each build you are in. Or write to suporte@casaderaiz.pt from the account’s own address, and it is deleted within 30 days. The email, the name and any build you are the last member of are deleted. If you run a build other people are in, you choose first whether to hand it to one of them or delete it for everyone. The build saved on your phone is untouched – if you want that gone too, uninstall the app, and export the backup before you do. The copy in a browser is deleted, with the session.

How do I make a backup?

Under the circle in the top-left corner, in Cópia de segurança (Backup), the app exports a single file containing every record and attached file, and hands it to the share sheet. Keep it off the phone – Files, iCloud Drive, email, wherever you prefer. On a shared build, attachments you have never opened on this phone are only in the cloud; before exporting, the app says how many there are and offers to download them first.

On a computer, in Definições → Cópia de segurança (Settings → Backup), the same file goes to the downloads folder. It is restored on a phone, not on the computer.

I changed phones. How do I bring the build with me?

It depends on where the build is.

  • If it is kept in the cloud, shared or not, it opens with your account – no backup and no invite code (from version 1.4). On the new phone, tap Tenho um convite para uma obra (I have an invite to a build) on the first screen, then Entrar ou criar conta (Sign in or create an account), and sign in with the same account. Go back to the first screen: Abrir (Open) appears with the build's name. Photos arrive as thumbnails, and each original when you open it.
  • If it is only on the phone, export the backup on the old device, install the app on the new one, and import the file from the same screen. Records and attachments both come back.

A build kept in the cloud is not moved with the backup: imported on the new phone, it becomes a separate copy that no longer receives anything from the other members or sends anything to them.

I deleted the app without a backup. Can you recover it?

If the build was only on the phone, no – and it isn't a matter of willingness: there is no copy of it on the developer's side. If iPhone backup was enabled, the iOS backup may have included the app's storage; that is the only possibility, and it is managed in your system settings. A build that was shared comes back when you install the app and sign in to the same account.

Does the app calculate IMT or Imposto do Selo?

No, and it never will. Both are banded, both vary by property type and region, and both change most years – a figure the app computed would be believed because it is a number. The app opens the field; you type what the assessment says.

Which VAT rate should I use?

The app offers the list of legal rates and lets you choose; it does not tell you which one applies to your case. That question is for your accountant, or for the contractor issuing the invoice. Nothing in this app is tax, legal or engineering advice.

Does it work offline?

It does. Everything you write is saved to the phone first, and nothing waits for a network to happen – on a site with no reception the app works just the same. A connection is only needed to create an account, to sign in, and for shared builds to catch up between phones.

On a computer, the page needs a connection to open. Once it is open, the same: what you write lands in the browser first and goes when there is a connection.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad.

What about Android?

Not for now. If that changes, it will be said here.
