Privacy policy

De Raiz — build control for people managing their own house.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Ler em português

An obra you keep to yourself never leaves your phone. An obra you choose to share with whoever is building it with you also lives on a server in the European Union, where only that obra's members can read it. You decide which of the two happens, obra by obra, and the whole app works with no account at all.

Sharing an obra is not possible in the current version of the app, so every obra is on the phone of whoever wrote it and nowhere else. The account already exists and is optional; what it holds is below.

What is collected

If you do not create an account: nothing

Without an account, the app does not collect, transmit or have access to any information about you. Everything you write stays in the app's private storage, on your device.

If you create an account

The account is optional and exists in order to share an obra. Creating one stores:

  • the email address you registered with, to identify the account, confirm the registration and reset the password;
  • the password, if you chose that method — stored irreversibly, never in readable text;
  • the identifier Apple or Google return, if you signed in with one of them, and the name those companies supplied. Apple often supplies no name at all, in which case no name is stored;
  • the dates the account was created and last signed in on, recorded by the authentication service.

Signing in sends nothing from your obra. Creating the account and signing in is all that happens; the quotes, the payments, the documents and the photos stay where they were.

If you share an obra

From the moment you choose to put an obra in the cloud — and only from that moment — that obra has a copy on the server: the records you wrote and the files you attached. That is what lets the same obra appear on the phone of whoever is building it with you. Any obra you do not put there still does not leave your device.

What still does not exist

  • usage analytics, telemetry or usage statistics;
  • automatic crash reporting;
  • advertising, ad tracking or advertising identifiers;
  • selling, sharing or handing your data to third parties for commercial purposes;
  • tracking your activity across other apps or other websites.

The app's developer receives no copies, summaries or statistics of what is in your obra, and does not open anybody's data to read it.

Where your data lives

On your device, always: the obra, its phases, the trades, the quotes, the payments, the suppliers, the documents and the photos are stored in the app's private storage. Files you attach are copied into that same private folder. The app was built to work this way — on a building site with no reception, everything keeps working.

And, for the account and any obra you share, on a server in the European Union. The service is Supabase, acting as processor, in the Central EU — Frankfurt, Germany region. The data does not leave the European Union. Supabase is also what sends the registration and password-reset emails.

Who can see a shared obra

That obra's members, and nobody else. This is not a matter of good intentions: every table on the server has row level security enabled — a rule the database itself applies on every read and every write, answering "nothing" to anyone who is not a member of that obra. It is not the app's code deciding what to show; it is the server refusing to hand it over.

You join an obra by being invited by whoever created it. Each member sees the other members' names — that is what the name is for — and nothing else about them. There are no public profiles, no user search, and no way to find out who has an account.

Beyond the members, access exists only for whoever hosts the service, under the processing agreement, and for the app's developer, strictly as far as needed to keep it running or to meet a legal obligation.

Backups you create

The app can export a single file containing all of your records and files. That file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet: you choose where it goes — Files, iCloud Drive, email, or another service.

Once you send it to a service, that service's own terms and privacy policy apply, and they are outside this app's control.

Separately, if you have iPhone backup enabled, iOS may include this app's private storage in that backup. That is a feature of Apple's operating system, managed in your device settings.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo to attach to the obra. The image is saved on the device, and leaves it only if the obra is shared.
  • Files — only when you choose a document to attach or a backup file to import. The app receives only the file you selected.

Choosing a photo from your library goes through the system's own picker, which hands over only the image you chose — the app never has access to your library.

Links out of the app

Some screens open things outside the app: a product address you saved, a supplier's phone number or email, and this page. In those cases your browser, phone or mail client takes over, and their rules apply.

Signing in with Apple or Google goes through those companies' own sign-in screens, and at that moment their policies apply too. If you would rather not involve them, you can create an account with an email address and a password alone.

How long it is kept

  • On your device, for as long as you want. Deleting the app deletes that data with it, and all that remains is whatever you exported or shared.
  • The account, for as long as it exists. Deleting it deletes the email, the name and the link to the obras it was a member of.
  • A shared obra, for as long as it has members. When the last member leaves it or deletes their account, the obra, its records and its files are deleted from the server. Leaving a shared obra does not delete the copy on your phone.

Legal basis

Processing the email address and the name rests on performing the service you asked for — the account and sharing do not work without them (GDPR, Article 6(1)(b)). There is no processing based on marketing interests, because there is no marketing. The controller is the person who develops the app, reachable at the address below.

Your rights

Over the data on your device, the control is entirely yours: you can see it, correct it, export it from the backup screen, and delete it by uninstalling the app. Nobody has to hand it to you, because it never left.

Over the data on the server, you have the right to:

  • know what is stored and access it;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • receive a copy in a readable format — the app's export does exactly that, and you do not need anybody's permission to run it;
  • delete the account and what belongs to it;
  • object to the processing and restrict it.

To delete the account, write to suporte@casaderaiz.pt from the account's own address. The email, the name and any obra you are the last member of are deleted, within 30 days. Export the backup before asking: once it is deleted there is nothing to recover it from.

If you believe your data is being handled improperly, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados.

Children

The app is for managing a private building project and is not directed at children. No children's data is requested or collected, and the account is not meant to be created by them.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever does something that requires changing this text, the text changes here, with the updated date above. A version that collects or transmits anything new has to say so on this page before doing it — that rule is what forced this page to be rewritten when the app gained accounts.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or to exercise any of the rights above: suporte@casaderaiz.pt
