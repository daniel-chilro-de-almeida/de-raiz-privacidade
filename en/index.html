De Raiz

Build control for people managing their own house.

Ler em português

A personal app for someone building their own house in Portugal and hiring the trades directly, with no general contractor. It answers four questions that come up every day:

  • What will this cost in total, and am I within budget?
  • What have I paid each supplier, and what is left?
  • Which phase am I in, and what must be handled before the next one?
  • Where is the contract, the invoice or the photo of that?

It collects no data at all. No accounts, no server, and it works offline — everything you enter stays on your device. The privacy policy spells it out.
