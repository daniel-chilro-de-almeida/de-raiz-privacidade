What it does

A tool for one build and for the people paying for it. It is not a platform for contractors, and it does not connect to your bank.

  • Quotes side by side

    Compare the quotes for each trade and award one. The contracted amount is then what counts.

  • VAT as you entered it

    With or without IVA, as the invoice has it. The legal rates are there to choose from.

  • The plot kept apart

    Price, IMT, stamp duty and escritura with a total of their own, outside the build’s budget.

  • Extra work

    What is added to or taken off the contract mid-build, with payments of its own.

  • Site diary

    What happened each day, with photos, for when someone asks why.

  • Reminders

    A notice on your phone before each payment and each deadline.

  • For the whole household

    Share the build with whoever is building it with you. Each records what they paid.

  • On a computer

    The same build on a big screen, at app.casaderaiz.pt.

  • Backup and export

    A full backup in one file, and CSV and PDF for whoever needs to see the accounts.

Your build, your data

No advertising. No tracking what you do. No crash reports.

Your build starts out saved on your phone. If you want to keep it to yourself, it never leaves. If you decide to share it, the data lives on a server in the European Union, and only the people in the build can get to it.

On the phone, an account is optional. On a computer, at app.casaderaiz.pt, you need an account to reach your builds through the cloud.

Privacy policy Support

The app provides no tax, legal or engineering advice. Phases and deadlines vary between municipalities: always confirm with your local council and the professionals involved. The figures on the screen above are from a fictional build.
